history_edu Heritage & Continuity

BEYOND TIME.
BEYOND EXPECTATIONS.

"True luxury is not simply measured in hours. It is defined by unwavering dedication, relentless precision, and an enduring commitment to perpetual excellence."

In 1926, Rolex created the Oyster — the world's first waterproof wristwatch — forever sealing the movement against dust, water, and atmospheric shock. Five years later came the Perpetual rotor, an ingenious self-winding mechanism that harnessed the natural movement of the wearer's wrist to power the escapement continuously.

Today, from our four manufacture sites across Switzerland, every single balance spring, gold alloy, and Cerachrom ceramic insert is developed and manufactured entirely in-house. This complete autonomy ensures that every timepiece bearing the Crown maintains unprecedented chronometric integrity.
